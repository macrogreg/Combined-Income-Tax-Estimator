# Combined Income Tax Estimator

An Excel spreadsheet to calculate all common US income taxes combined together.

## Benefits over other tax calculators

This calculator **quickly** estimates your total federal tax liability.  
It properly combines the **most common tax types** for employed individuals **in one place**.  
Most other tax estimators focus only on one tax type (e.g., federal income tax only), or ask a ton of detailed questions that take forever to answer.

Included taxes:

- Federal Income Tax (including Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT))
- Long-Term Capital Gains Tax
- Social Security Tax
- Medicare Taxes (including Additional Medicare Tax and NIIT)

## Disclaimer

**⚠️ This calculator is not a qualified tax advice.**

This tool is for estimation purposes only. It should be reasonably accurate for ~90% of employed individuals.  
The calculator accounts for common income types, such as W-2, capital gains, rental income, and others.

For fully reliable tax calculations, especially for complex tax situations, always consult a qualified tax professional!
